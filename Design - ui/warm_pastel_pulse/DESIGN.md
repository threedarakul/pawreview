## Brand & Style
This design system pairs the warmth, approachability, and gentleness of whimsical storybook illustration with the disciplined precision of modern enterprise software. Designed for internal tools, employee performance tracking, and human resources workflows, it reframes high-stakes appraisal processes into encouraging, calm, and collaborative touchpoints.

The style merges soft tactile UI with clean modern SaaS layouts. Rather than cold, utilitarian enterprise aesthetics, the environment is defined by creamy ivory base surfaces, sunlit coral call-to-actions, delicate pastel markers, and grounded charcoal typography. The emotional impact is reassuring, hospitable, and delightfully organized—diminishing anxiety around deadlines and evaluations while maintaining crisp legibility and high information density.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in soft warmth and high-contrast accessibility:

- **Primary (`#FF7043`):** Radiant warm coral/peach. Used for primary calls-to-action, key performance highlights, interactive focus rings, and milestone celebrations.
- **Secondary (`#89C4F4`):** Gentle baby blue. Provides supportive accents, information banners, progress bars, and passive category markers.
- **Tertiary (`#F8BBD0`):** Soft pastel rose. Functions as an emotive accent for team feedback, commendations, and decorative avatar frames.
- **Neutral (`#2D2A26`):** Warm deep charcoal. Replaces pure harsh blacks to provide high-legibility typographic hierarchy while preserving the cozy paper-like atmosphere.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF4EC` (Warm Cream)
- **Container / Card Surface:** `#FDFBF7` (Soft Pearl Ivory)
- **Subtle Outline / Border:** `#EFE7DC` (Muted Linen)

### Status & Review Tokens
- **Normal / Healthy (`#81C784` / Mint Green):** Completed evaluations, regular tracking pace.
- **Upcoming Review (`#FFD54F` / Warm Amber Gold):** Deadlines within 7 days, intermediate warnings.
- **Overdue / Action Needed (`#FF5252` / Soft Coral Crimson):** Overdue performance checkpoints, escalations.

## Typography
Typographic personality balances structural geometry with humanist softness. 

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its friendly rounded bowls, sturdy modern architecture, and energetic optimism. Heavy display weights (`700` and `800`) deliver warm editorial presence across hero banners and review scorecards.
- **Body (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Offers clear letterforms, open apertures, and exceptional readability at dense sizes. It prevents visual fatigue when reading extended 360-degree performance reviews or detailed employee feedback.
- **Labels & Tags (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** High-weight labels ensure critical metrics, status chips, and tabular headers remain crisp and legible against creamy backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid system for desktop dashboards (breakpoints at 1280px and 1024px) scaling down to 4 columns on mobile viewports (<768px). 

Rhythm is deliberate and spacious:
- Dashboard layouts enforce a 2rem outer canvas margin, framing panels like clean pages in an executive binder.
- Component groupings utilize `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled form elements and `space-md` (16px) for intra-card content sections.
- Sections and dashboard cards maintain a minimum internal padding of `space-lg` (24px) to preserve a relaxed, uncrowded atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is realized through layered pastel planes, subtle warm ambient lighting, and gentle organic boundary lines rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** Canvas `#FAF4EC`. Flat matte base.
- **Card Containers (Elevation 1):** `#FDFBF7` bordered by a faint linen stroke (`1px solid #EFE7DC`), accompanied by an ambient warm shadow: `0 8px 24px -6px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive Floating Elements (Elevation 2):** Modals, tooltips, and active popovers use soft layered shadows with a hint of coral diffusion: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(255, 112, 67, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.04)`.
- **Active / Pressed State:** Inner inset warm glow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.06)`), providing a soft, tactile, pillowy physical press response.

## Shapes
Roundedness sits at Level 2, balancing structured SaaS card ergonomics with rounded warmth:

- Standard interactive controls, form inputs, and small tiles inherit `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radiuses.
- Content cards, modals, and metric review panels utilize `1rem` (`16px`) or `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- Badges, status chips, and button calls-to-action leverage full pill silhouettes (`9999px`) to mirror the playful, friendly, rounded nature of the brand.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** High-impact `#FF7043` solid background with pure white text, fully rounded (`rounded-full`), padded with `12px 28px`. On hover: subtle shift to `#FF8B5E` with `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(255, 112, 67, 0.35)` elevation.
- **Secondary Pill:** Cream ivory surface with a solid 2px outline in `#FF7043`, text colored in `#FF7043`. Hover transitions to a 10% tinted coral fill (`rgba(255, 112, 67, 0.08)`).
- **Subtle / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `#2D2A26` text, transitioning on hover to a soft linen surface (`#EFE7DC`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Designed as compact pill tags (`rounded-full`, height 24px–28px, padded `4px 12px`, with bold `label-sm` font).
- **On-Track / Completed:** Soft mint background (`#E8F5E9`) with deep forest green text (`#2E7D32`) and a small solid mint dot.
- **Upcoming Review (≤ 7 Days):** Soft amber background (`#FFF8E1`) with rich bronze text (`#F57F17`).
- **Overdue / Action Needed:** Pastel coral-blush background (`#FFEBEE`) with vibrant coral red text (`#C62828`).

### Cards & HR Review Containers
- Built on ivory `#FDFBF7` with `rounded-xl` corners and `1px solid #EFE7DC`.
- Metric scorecards feature pastel accent header banners (e.g., Baby Blue `#89C4F4` or Pastel Rose `#F8BBD0` at 15% opacity) to categorize review criteria: "Self Evaluation", "Peer Feedback", and "Manager Sign-off".

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` background set against cream surfaces, bordered with `1.5px solid #EFE7DC`, rounded to `0.5rem`. Placeholder text in warm muted gray (`#8C877D`). Focus state applies a crisp `#FF7043` border with a 3px outer glow ring of `rgba(255, 112, 67, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smooth pillowy squares and circles with a 2px boundary. Checked state uses solid `#FF7043` fill with a bold white checkmark.

### Review Timeline & Avatar Displays
- Review tracker milestones use continuous dashed connecting lines in `#EFE7DC`. Active steps are capped with solid coral pill icons.
- Employee profile avatars are encased in dual-ring borders: an inner `#FDFBF7` spacer and an outer pastel peach or sky-blue accent ring.
